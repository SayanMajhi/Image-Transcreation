\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{ch:conclusion}
\section{Conclusion}
The project implements an inspectable, guarded pipeline for culture-aware image-editing research. Its strongest confirmed result is engineering reliability: saved outputs are traceable, bounded edits can preserve exterior and protected pixels exactly, and the Phase 9 compositing workflow replays deterministically across six cases. The project does not establish automatic cultural transcreation. Diffusion outputs were not visually convincing, and the repeatability pilot's visual assessment is incomplete.

\section{Future work}
Priority next steps are: (i) obtain genuine blinded human reviews under the frozen protocol; (ii) evaluate scenes with textured or cluttered backgrounds; (iii) improve reference matching for camera angle, lighting and native contact shadows; (iv) test higher-capacity editors only with declared hardware and model provenance; and (v) investigate planner-to-review handoff without weakening the approval boundary.
