usage: cli.py [-h] [--version] {caption,plan,ground} ...

Culture transcreation research CLI.

positional arguments:
  {caption,plan,ground}
    caption             Caption one image with SmolVLM-500M
    plan                Plan cultural edits with local Ollama; never modifies
                        images
    ground              Research-only text localization; never executes
                        planner edits

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --version             show program's version number and exit
