\chapter*{Declaration on Assistance and Reproducibility}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Declaration on Assistance and Reproducibility}
The software, experiments and write-ups include AI coding-assistant support under the project author's direction. The report distinguishes assistant observations, user feedback and independently checkable program output. Reproduction commands, saved configurations, hashes and test logs are retained in the repository. This statement does not substitute for an institute-specific academic-integrity declaration.
